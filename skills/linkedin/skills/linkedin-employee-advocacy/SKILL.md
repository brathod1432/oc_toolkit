# LinkedIn Employee Advocacy

## When to use
- User wants to activate their team to share company content on LinkedIn
- User needs a structured advocacy program with roles, cadence, and governance
- User wants template posts that employees can personalize without sounding robotic
- User is a marketing manager or people-ops lead building a systematic program

## Core components

**1. Governance playbook.** What employees can and can't say. Who approves content. How to handle sensitive topics (funding, legal, HR). What to do when someone asks a hard question in comments.

**2. Content cadence.** How often each employee is asked to post or share. Types: amplify (reshare company post with comment), original (write their own story). Typical cadence: 1-2 amplify + 0-1 original per week.

**3. Template library.** Ready-to-use post starters employees fill in with their own experience. Templates are starting points — they must be personalized, not copy-pasted.

**4. Team matrix.** Which employees post in which role/pillar (engineering → technical depth, sales → customer story, leadership → vision, people → culture).

## Steps

1. **Understand the program.** Ask: team size, target audience, primary business goal (brand awareness / hiring / sales pipeline / thought leadership), existing LinkedIn activity levels.
2. **Define roles and pillars.** Map employee functions to content pillars. Not every employee needs to post — start with 5-10 willing advocates.
3. **Build the governance doc.** Cover: approved topics, topics requiring approval, how to respond to comments, escalation paths, what not to say.
4. **Create the cadence matrix.** Weekly schedule by employee tier (active advocate / passive amplifier / opt-out).
5. **Draft 5-10 template posts.** Each template has: fill-in-the-blank structure, the pillar it serves, the formula used, estimated time to personalize (target: 10 min per post).
6. **Launch plan.** Internal announcement, training session agenda, first-week task list, tracking method (simple spreadsheet or Notion table).

## Template format (each)

```
TEMPLATE: [Pillar Name] — [Formula Code]
Estimated personalization time: ~10 min

HOOK (fill in):
[YOUR SPECIFIC OUTCOME/SITUATION] changed [thing] for me in [context].

BODY (fill in your experience):
[What you did / what you saw / what surprised you]
[The specific detail only you know — a number, a name, a date]
[What you'd tell someone earlier in their journey]

CLOSE:
[Question for your audience OR soft CTA]

NOTES FOR PERSONALIZING:
- Replace [YOUR SPECIFIC OUTCOME] with something you actually experienced
- Keep the numbers real — readers spot invented metrics immediately
- Add your name to any internal project references
```

## Hard rules
Global voice rules: see root SKILL.md §Voice rules.
- Templates are starters, not scripts. Every employee post must have at least one personalized element.
- Governance doc must be reviewed and approved by HR/Legal before launch.
- Never post internal financial data, customer names (without permission), or HR-sensitive information.
- Employees always post as themselves, not as the company brand voice.
